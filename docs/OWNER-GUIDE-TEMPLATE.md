Use your **laptop**. (The editor doesn't open on phones – that's on purpose.)

### Open the editor
1. Click the **Website Editor** bookmark.
2. If it asks you to sign in: [type your email → it emails you a 6-digit code → type the code].
   You stay signed in for about a month.

### You edit right on your website
The editor opens on **your own website** with your unpublished changes. Anything you can change has a dark
**✎ Change …** button (small things like a logo get a round **✎**). Click it (or the thing itself) and a panel opens
on the right – your page shows a little smaller beside it. **Done** closes the panel.
* **Every change shows on the page straight away.** Nothing goes on the real website until you click **Publish**.
* **Page:** the buttons (or the list) at the top move between your pages. (Clicking a link's words in the page changes
  the words; the little box that opens has an **Open** button to go to that page.)
* The bar at the bottom: **See it on: Computer / Phone** shows the phone version. **Publish** → **Yes, publish**:
  it says **Publishing… about 2 minutes**, then **✓ Done – view your website**. **Undo all changes** puts everything
  back to what's on the website now.
* Typing words or a review and clicked something else? The editor asks before anything you typed is lost.

### Change any wording
Every word on your website can be changed – headings, paragraphs, buttons, the menu, the top and the bottom of the page,
your phone number and email.
1. Go to the page and **click the words** you want to change (a dotted line and a small **✎** show when the mouse is over them).
2. **Type** your new words right there. In paragraphs, **Enter** starts a new paragraph.
3. Click **Save – show it on my page** (for a short line, **Enter** saves too). **Cancel** puts the old words back.
4. When you're happy, click **Publish**.
* Menu items and buttons: only the words change – they still go to the same place.
* Your phone number and email must stay a real phone number / email address (the Call and Email buttons use them).
* Some lines **must stay on your website** (for example [your brokerage / license line]): you can change the words, but not remove them.
* Words only on the footer pages or the "page not found" page: choose them under **More pages…** at the top.

### Change a photo
Go to that page and click **✎ Change photo** on it → **Replace photo** (or drag a photo onto it in the panel).
Changed your mind? **Undo**. Then **Publish**.

### Change the slideshow at the top of your homepage
On the Home page click **✎ Change slideshow**.
* **Your photos** come first: **+ Add photos** (or drag photos onto the box). **⠿ Drag** to change the order:
  **Photo 1 shows first**. **Which part matters most?** keeps that part in view on phones.
* Then choose **Slideshow** (photos take turns) or **One still photo** (only Photo 1 shows).
* **Speed:** Slow, **Normal** (recommended) or Fast. **How photos change:** **Slide** (recommended) or Fade.
* Then **Publish**.

### Add, change or remove a review
* **Fix one:** on the reviews page click **✎ Change review** on it, change it, then **Save – show it on my page**.
* **Add or remove:** click **✎ Change reviews – add, change or remove** → **+ Add a review**: paste the client's words, add a short title
  and the date. The name is optional; only add it if the client is OK with it. A new review goes to the top.
  **Remove** takes one off.
* Then **Publish**.

### Change the order of reviews
Click **✎ Change reviews – add, change or remove**. **Review 1 shows first** (on the reviews page and on the homepage).
* Click **Move up** or **Move down** on a review, or hold **⠿ Drag** and move it where you want it.
* Each move is saved straight away and your page shows it. Then **Publish**.

**Prefer the old screen?** Click **List view** at the top right of the editor.
**The page says "You were signed out"?** Click **Sign in again**, sign in, come back and click **Try again**.

### If something goes wrong
* **Nothing you do in the editor is live until you click Publish.** Not happy? Click **Undo my unpublished changes**.
* "Changed on another computer" → click **Reload latest**, then try again.
* Anything else → text [Your name]. Your work is saved.
